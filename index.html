№ 01 Manifesto Est. MMXXV

There's nothing quite like being let down by someone you love.

It's not that you're expecting perfection. It's just that you see the potential. You know how great it could all be with the right amount of effort, care, and maybe even passion.

Rebrief is a love letter to Canadian advertising. But it's the kind of love letter that comes with a nudge to do better. Not because we're disappointed. But because untapped potential is almost as frustrating as no potential at all.

Canada. A beacon of multiculturalism, a masterclass in PR outranking reality, the first-ranked country of the sixth-ranked sport (behind soccer, cricket, basketball, baseball and football, let's be real) and, stunningly, home to some of the best advertising talent and history on the planet earth.

There is something unique, here.

The mix of cultures, influences, and inferiority complexes has led Canada to have a voice in the world of advertising that is almost as big as the one we have in entertainment. This is an attempt to understand, appreciate, and elevate that voice.

Rebrief. A Canadian journal for those who take advertising seriously enough to love it, and those who love it enough to take it seriously.

The Canadian advertising industry faces a critical moment: consolidation, budget pressures, creative fatigue, and technological disruption have created an opportunity for ambitious reinvention.

№ 03 Sponsorship A Few Good Agencies
A Feature · Six decades of agency self-promotion

The return of the agency house ad.

At one point, the "House Ad" was the sharpest piece of self-promotion — and critique — an agency could make. We're advertising nerds. It's time to bring it back.

"I'm just reading this for the Ads!"

How long has it been since any of the awards, the hype videos, or the LBB articles we all write about ourselves had an edge? The House Ad is a dream for your creative team — and Issue One's timeline is tight, so just go for it and try to "win" the issue. We're seeding the mag to marketers and potential clients. What better recruitment tool when it's time to hire up?
