§ Why Rebrief The case & what you're backing

Canadian advertising needs a Rebrief. The industry faces a critical moment — consolidation, creative fatigue, and technological disruption have created an opportunity for ambitious reinvention.

Rebrief is that publication. Print-forward, not-for-profit, built by working strategists, creatives, and designers who wanted something to read that took the job seriously without taking itself seriously.

§ Feature The Agency House Ad

The Return of the Agency House Ad.

The decline of print media means fewer agency house ads. As much as we love the annual awards videos, the house ad used to be the sharpest way an agency could pitch themselves. They also used to be the best projects.

We treat house ads as part of the Rebrief experience. We offer two sizes: a full page and a small 1/12th page "classified" style ad. We truly want people to pick up Rebrief as much for the ads as the articles.

§ FAQ Common Questions
Is sponsorship tax-deductible?
No. Rebrief Magazine Society is incorporated as a not-for-profit, not a registered charity. Sponsorships should be expensed as marketing spend, not charitable contributions.
What's the editorial review process for house ads?
We reserve the right to decline artwork that's outside the spirit of the publication. If we have concerns, we'll flag them early and work with you.
Can one sponsor buy multiple Backpages slots?
Yes. Stack them for a larger placement or split them across messages. We're easy.
When do I get my copies and event tickets?
Copies are delivered the week of the launch event. Event tickets are issued in early June once logistics are finalized.
What if the issue ships late?
We'll ship by Summer 2026. If any delay arises, sponsors are notified first, and the terms are adjusted accordingly. We'd rather miss a date than ship something we're not proud of.
Can I sponsor Issue Two without sponsoring Issue One?
Yes — but Issue One sponsors hold first right of refusal on their tier. Available slots will be offered publicly after Issue One sponsors have committed.
